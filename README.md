## Mission

Replace expensive SaaS tools with free alternatives. Every resource in this collection is vetted, categorized, and ranked so you can build, deploy, and scale without burning through your budget.

Whether you are a student shipping your first side project, an indie hacker bootstrapping a startup, or a team evaluating cost-effective tooling, this repo saves you thousands of dollars per year.

## Contributing

We welcome contributions from the community. Each resource submission must include:

- ✅ **Category** – Where it belongs
- ✅ **Free tier details** – What is actually free
- ✅ **Verification status** – Tested / Community Verified / Unverified
- ✅ **Card requirement** – Whether a credit card is required
- ✅ **Region restrictions** – Global / country-specific

Please read our [Contributing Guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting.
