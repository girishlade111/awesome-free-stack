# Indie Hacker Learning Path

Learn to build and launch profitable solo projects — from idea to first paying customer — using only free tools.

## Phase 1: Mindset & Validation

| Topic | Free Resource | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Idea Validation | [Lean Startup](http://theleanstartup.com) | 1 week |
| Market Research | [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com) | 1 week |
| Competitor Analysis | BuiltWith (partial free), manual research | 1 week |
| Landing Page | [Vercel + Next.js](https://vercel.com) | 1 week |

### Tools
- **Waitlist**: ConvertKit (free up to 1k subs)
- **Forms**: Tally.so (free)
- **Survey**: Google Forms (free)
- **Analytics**: Plausible (30-day trial) or Umami (self-host)
- **Domain**: `is-a.dev` (free subdomain)

| Topic | Free Resource | Duration |

### Free Launch Platforms
- **Product Hunt**: Free to launch
- **Hacker News**: Free to submit (build audience first)
- **Reddit**: Free (subreddits like r/SideProject, r/alphaandbetausers)
- **Indie Hackers**: Free to post
- **BetaList**: Free listing
