## Turso

**Website**: https://turso.tech
**Docs**: https://docs.turso.tech
**GitHub**: https://github.com/tursodatabase/turso
**Category**: databases

### Description
Edge SQLite database built on libsql. Deploy database replicas globally for instant reads with low-latency writes. Ideal for serverless and edge applications.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 500 MB
- **Row reads**: 1 billion/month
- **Row writes**: 250 million/month
- **MAU**: 25K
- **Locations**: 10+

### Paid Plan
- **Team**: $29/month
- **Storage**: 8 GB
- **MAU**: 10K
- **Locations**: 25
- **Features**: Team workspace, priority support
