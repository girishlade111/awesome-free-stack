### Description
Serverless Postgres platform with built-in full-text search, analytics, and AI vector capabilities. Declarative database branching and column-level permissions for collaborative development.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 15 GB
- **Records**: 5K
- **Branches**: 2
- **Features**: Built-in search, vector capabilities, REST API

### Paid Plan
- **Pro**: $20/month
- **Storage**: 50 GB
- **Records**: 100K
- **Branches**: 10
- **Features**: Priority support
