## Neon

**Website**: https://neon.tech
**Docs**: https://neon.tech/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/neondatabase/neon
**Category**: databases
**Subcategory**: sql

### Description
Serverless PostgreSQL with instant provisioning, branching, and autoscaling. Separate compute and storage with pay-per-use pricing. Generous free tier ideal for side projects.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 500 MB
- **Compute**: 100 hours/month
- **Projects**: 1
- **Branches**: 10
- **Bandwidth**: 1 GB/month

### Paid Plan
- **Launch**: $19/month
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **Compute**: 500 hours/month
- **Branches**: 50
- **Features**: Team collaboration, IP allowlisting
