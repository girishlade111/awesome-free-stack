## Aiven

**Website**: https://aiven.io

**GitHub**: https://github.com/aiven

### Description
Managed open-source database platform supporting PostgreSQL, MySQL, Redis, Kafka, Cassandra, and OpenSearch. Deploy on your cloud of choice (AWS, GCP, Azure) with high availability.

### Free Tier
- **PostgreSQL/MySQL**: 1 GB storage, 1 node
- **Redis**: 256 MB, 1 node
- **Kafka**: 1 GB
- **Note**: Free plans have limited availability

### Paid Plan
- **Startup**: $39/month
- **RAM**: 2 GB
- **Nodes**: 2 (HA)
- **Storage**: 10 GB
