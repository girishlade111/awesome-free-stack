## PlanetScale

**Website**: https://planetscale.com
**Docs**: https://planetscale.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/planetscale
**Category**: databases

### Description
Serverless MySQL platform built on Vitess. Known for branching workflows, non-blocking schema changes, and generous free tier. Database branching makes schema migrations as safe as code deploys.

### Free Tier
- **Database**: 1
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **Production branches**: 1
- **Dev branches**: 3
- **Reads**: 1 billion rows/month
- **Writes**: 10 million rows/month

- **Production branches**: 10
- **Reads**: 100 billion rows/month
