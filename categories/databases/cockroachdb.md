## CockroachDB

**Website**: https://www.cockroachlabs.com
**Docs**: https://www.cockroachlabs.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/cockroachdb/cockroach
**Category**: databases
**Subcategory**: sql

### Description
Distributed SQL database with PostgreSQL compatibility. Scales horizontally across regions with strong consistency and resilient auto-healing. Serverless free tier with generous limits.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 5 GB
- **Request Units**: 250 million/month
- **Clusters**: 1
- **Retention**: 30 days

### Paid Plan
- **Standard**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Storage**: $0.20/GB/month
- **RUs**: $0.005/million
