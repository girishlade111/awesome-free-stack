### Description
Leading cloud platform in Asia-Pacific with strong presence in China. Offers 30+ services including compute, databases, AI/ML, and CDN. $300 free trial credits.

### Free Tier
- **Trial**: $300 credits for 30-90 days
- **Compute**: 1 ecs.t6-c1m1.large (1vCPU, 1GB)
- **Storage**: 5GB OSS (Object Storage)
- **Serverless**: 1M function calls/month
- **Tier type**: Trial + limited free tier

### Paid Plan
- **Starting at**: $0.01/h (ECS instance)
- **Reserved instances**: Up to 40% discount
- **Billing**: Usage-based per hour
