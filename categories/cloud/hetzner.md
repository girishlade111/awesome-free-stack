## Hetzner

**Website**: https://www.hetzner.com

### Description
German cloud provider known for best-in-class price-to-performance ratio. Offers cloud VMs, dedicated servers, and managed storage. European data centers with strong GDPR compliance.

### Free Tier
- **No free tier**: Pay-as-you-go only
- **Promotions**: Occasional €20 promotional credits
- **Minimum**: €3.49/month for CX11

### Paid Plan
- **Cloud CX11**: €3.49/month (1vCPU, 2GB RAM, 20GB SSD)
- **Dedicated**: Starting at €35/month
- **Storage Box**: €3.49/month
- **Billing**: Hourly or monthly
