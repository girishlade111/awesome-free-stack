### Description
Free .us top-level domain registration for US citizens and permanent residents. Part of the US Department of Commerce program. A real TLD completely free of charge — domain registration, renewal, and DNS management included.

### Free Tier
- **Domain**: Free .us domain
- **Eligibility**: US citizens or permanent residents only
- **Address**: Must provide valid US address
- **Renewal**: Free annual renewal
- **DNS**: Included

### Paid Plan
- **Price**: No paid version
- **Model**: Free for eligible US residents
