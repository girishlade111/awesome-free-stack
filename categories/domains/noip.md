## No-IP

**Website**: https://www.noip.com
**Docs**: https://www.noip.com/support
**GitHub**: N/A
**Category**: domains
**Subcategory**: dynamic-dns

### Description
Pioneering dynamic DNS provider offering free subdomains for home servers and self-hosting. Creates persistent hostnames (like `myserver.ddns.net`) that follow your changing IP address.

### Free Tier
- **Hostnames**: 3
- **Domains**: ddns.net, hopto.org, noip.com, and others
- **Confirmation**: Required every 30 days
- **DNS records**: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT

### Paid Plan
- **Enhanced**: $24.95/year
- **Features**: 25 hostnames, no confirmation, email support, domain registration
