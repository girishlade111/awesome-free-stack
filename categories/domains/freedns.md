## FreeDNS (afraid.org)

### Description
Community-powered free DNS and subdomain service. Access thousands of free subdomains contributed by volunteers, or bring your own domain and get free DNS management with dynamic DNS support.

### Free Tier
- **Subdomains**: 5 free from community pool
- **DNS zones**: Unlimited for your own domains
- **Record types**: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, TXT, SRV
- **Dynamic DNS**: API-based updates
- **Shared domains**: 1000s available from community

### Paid Plan
- **Price**: No paid version
- **Model**: Donation-supported
