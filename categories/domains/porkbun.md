### Description
Domain registrar with free DNS hosting, free WHOIS privacy (ID Protect), and competitive pricing. Known for quirky branding but serious infrastructure. SSL certificates and email hosting included with domains.

### Free Tier
- **DNS**: Free DNS hosting with every domain
- **Privacy**: Free WHOIS privacy (ID Protect) on all domains
- **SSL**: Free SSL via Let's Encrypt
- **Email**: Free email forwarding

### Paid Plan
- **Domains**: Starting at ~$1/year for TLDs like .xyz, .click
- **Renewals**: Standard renewal rates
