### Description
The most well-known free domain provider offering .tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, and .gq TLDs at no cost. Free for 12 months with free renewal if domain meets minimum traffic requirements. DNS management included.

**Note:** Has faced reliability issues since 2023. New registrations may be limited. Use cautiously for non-critical projects.

### Free Tier
- **Domains**: Free .tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, .gq
- **Duration**: 12 months (renewable free with usage)
- **DNS**: Free DNS management
- **Forwarding**: URL forwarding included

### Paid Plan
- **Price**: Standard renewal fees if not meeting usage requirements
- **Premium**: Paid premium domains available
