## Applitools Eyes

**Website**: https://applitools.com
**Docs**: https://applitools.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/applitools
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
AI-powered visual testing and monitoring platform. Uses Ultrafast Grid for cross-browser testing with visual AI that mimics human eyes. Detects layout shifts, missing elements, and visual regressions across 40+ browsers.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 1,000 test checkpoints/month, unlimited users, community support
- Cross-browser testing with Ultrafast Grid.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $99/month
- Plans from $99/month for 5K checkpoints. Enterprise with AI analytics, SLA, and dedicated support.
