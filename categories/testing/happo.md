## Happo

**Website**: https://happo.io
**Docs**: https://docs.happo.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/happo
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
Visual regression testing service for cross-browser screenshots. Renders components across Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Integrates with Storybook, Playwright, and Cypress. Free tier for small teams.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 1 project, 500 screenshots/month, 1 user, community support
- Cross-browser rendering included.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $59/month
- Team from $59/month for 3 projects, 3K screenshots, unlimited users. Enterprise with SSO and custom pricing.
