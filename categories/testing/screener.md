## Screener.io

**Website**: https://screener.io
**Docs**: https://screener.io/docs
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
Visual regression testing platform by SmartBear for component and full-page testing. Runs in browsers with pixel-level comparison, responsive testing, and cross-browser visual validation. Integrates with Selenium and Storybook.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 1 project, 1 user, 1,000 screenshots/month
- Cross-browser and responsive visual testing included.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $89/month
- Team from $89/month for 5 users and 5K screenshots. Enterprise with SSO, RBAC, and premium support.
