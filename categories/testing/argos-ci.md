## Argos CI

**Website**: https://argos-ci.com
**Docs**: https://argos-ci.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/argos-ci/argos
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
Open-source visual regression testing service for developers. Screenshots are compared pixel-by-pixel with AI-powered diff detection. Integrates with Playwright, Cypress, Puppeteer, and Storybook. Free for open source.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: unlimited screenshots for open source projects
- 1,000 screenshots/month for private repos
- Community support.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $79/month
- Pro from $79/month for 10K screenshots. Enterprise with SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support.
