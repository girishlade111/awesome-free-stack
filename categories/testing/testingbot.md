## TestingBot

**Website**: https://testingbot.com
**Docs**: https://testingbot.com/support/
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: browser-testing

### Description
Cloud browser and mobile testing platform with Selenium and Appium support. Provides real iOS and Android devices, 5,000+ browser combinations, and parallel testing. Free trial with monthly testing minutes.

### Free Tier
- Free trial: 200 minutes/month live testing, 6 parallel tests, all browser and device combinations included.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $25/month
- Plans from $25/month for 400 min and 2 parallel tests. Enterprise with dedicated devices and support.
