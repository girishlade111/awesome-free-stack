## Chromatic

**Website**: https://www.chromatic.com
**Docs**: https://www.chromatic.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/chromaui/chromatic-cli
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
Visual regression testing for Storybook components. Automatically captures screenshots of every Storybook story and compares against baselines. Integrates with CI/CD and provides UI review workflows for teams.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 5,000 snapshots/month, unlimited projects, 1 user
- Includes Storybook integration and CI pipeline support.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $149/month
- Team from $149/month for 3 users and 50K snapshots. Enterprise with SSO, dedicated support, and advanced features.
