## Percy

**Website**: https://percy.io
**Docs**: https://docs.percy.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/percy
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: visual-regression

### Description
Visual regression testing platform by BrowserStack. Integrates with CI/CD to capture screenshots, compare with baselines, and highlight pixel-level diffs. Supports web, mobile, and component-level visual testing.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 1 user, unlimited projects, 5,000 snapshots/month
- Includes parallel builds and GitHub/GitLab CI integration.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $49/month
- Team from $49/month for 5 users and 10K snapshots. Enterprise with SAML SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support.
