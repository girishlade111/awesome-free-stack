## LambdaTest

**Website**: https://www.lambdatest.com
**Docs**: https://www.lambdatest.com/support/docs/
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: browser-testing

### Description
Cloud cross-browser testing platform with 3,000+ browsers and real devices. Supports live interactive, automated Selenium/Cypress/Playwright testing, and visual regression. Free tier with monthly testing minutes.

### Free Tier
- Free for life plan: 60 min/month live testing, 30 min/month automated testing, 6 parallel sessions, 10 screenshot tests.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $15/month
- Live plans from $15/month for 2 parallel sessions. Automate plans from $99/month for 25 parallel tests.
