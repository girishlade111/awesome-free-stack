## CrossBrowserTesting

**Website**: https://crossbrowsertesting.com
**Docs**: https://crossbrowsertesting.com/documentation
**Category**: testing
**Subcategory**: browser-testing

### Description
SmartBear's cloud cross-browser testing platform with 2,050+ real browsers. Supports live interactive, automated Selenium testing, and visual screenshot comparison. Integrates with CI/CD pipelines.

### Free Tier
- Free trial: 100 minutes of automated testing and 30 minutes of live testing with all browser/OS combinations.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $49/month
- Plans from $49/month for 2 parallel tests and 60-min sessions. Enterprise with dedicated account management.
