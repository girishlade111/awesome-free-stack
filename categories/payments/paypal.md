## PayPal

**Website**: https://www.paypal.com
**Docs**: https://developer.paypal.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/paypal
**Category**: payments
**Subcategory**: payment-processing

### Description
The world's most widely used online payment platform with 400M+ active users. Accept payments via PayPal balance, credit/debit cards, and 25+ local payment methods in 200+ countries. Trusted brand with built-in buyer and seller protection.

### Free Tier
- **Monthly fee**: None
- **Setup fee**: None
- **Transaction fee**: 2.99% + $0.49 per transaction (US domestic)
- **Included**: Free PayPal.Me payment links, invoices, business account, mobile card reader

### Paid Plan
- **Merchant rates**: Lower rates for high-volume businesses
- **Features**: Custom checkout, advanced fraud protection, PayPal Working Capital
