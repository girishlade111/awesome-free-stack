## Razorpay

**Website**: https://razorpay.com
**Docs**: https://razorpay.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/razorpay
**Category**: payments
**Subcategory**: payment-processing

### Description
India's leading payment gateway with 100+ payment modes including UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, EMIs, and digital wallets. Built for Indian businesses with GST invoicing, Route (payouts), and subscription management.

### Free Tier
- **Setup fee**: Zero
- **Annual fee**: Zero
- **Transaction fee**: 2% + GST per transaction
- **Included**: Free payment pages, invoices, dashboard, and mobile app

### Paid Plan
- **Enterprise**: Custom rates for high-volume businesses
- **Features**: Dedicated account manager, priority support, custom settlement
