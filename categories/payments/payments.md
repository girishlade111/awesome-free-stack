## Selection Guide

- **Stripe** — Best for global SaaS, subscriptions, and complex payment flows
- **Razorpay** — Best for India-focused businesses (UPI, net banking, EMIs)
- **Paddle** — Best if you want full tax/VAT compliance handled (merchant of record)
- **Lemon Squeezy** — Best for digital products with license key management
- **PayPal** — Best for maximum buyer reach and brand trust

## Categories Covered

- **payment-processing**: Stripe, Razorpay, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, PayPal
- **invoicing**: All platforms include free invoicing
- **subscription-management**: Stripe, Paddle, Razorpay
- **checkout**: All platforms include hosted checkout
- **fraud-detection**: Stripe Radar, PayPal Seller Protection
