## Paddle

**Website**: https://www.paddle.com
**Docs**: https://developer.paddle.com

### Description
Merchant of record payment platform that handles global taxes, VAT, and compliance. Sell software globally with one integration — Paddle manages invoices, receipts, tax reporting, and fraud protection. No need to register for VAT in every country.

### Free Tier
- **Transaction fee**: 5% + $0.50 per transaction
- **Monthly fee**: None
- **Setup fee**: None
- **Included**: Full tax compliance, fraud protection, customer management, hosted checkout

### Paid Plan
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing for high-volume sellers
- **Features**: Volume discounts, dedicated support
