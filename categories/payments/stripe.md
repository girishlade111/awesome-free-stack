## Stripe

**Website**: https://stripe.com
**Docs**: https://stripe.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/stripe
**Category**: payments
**Subcategory**: payment-processing

### Description
Global payment processing platform. Accept payments, manage subscriptions, handle invoicing, and detect fraud. Industry-standard API for online payments with no monthly fees free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Transaction fee**: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful card charge
- **Monthly fee**: $0 — no monthly fees, no setup fees, no minimums
- **Connect**: Free tier for platforms with micro-transactions
- **Invoicing**: Free for up to 25 invoices/month

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Per-transaction pricing
- **Volume discounts**: Available for high-revenue businesses
- **Billing**: Transaction-fee based
