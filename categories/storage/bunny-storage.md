## Bunny Storage

**Website**: https://bunny.net/storage
**Docs**: https://docs.bunny.net/docs/storage-getting-started
**GitHub**: None
**Category**: storage
**Subcategory**: cdn-storage

### Description
Edge-optimized storage integrated with Bunny CDN. 10 GB free tier with 250 GB bandwidth/month. Files are replicated at edge locations for low-latency global delivery.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **Bandwidth**: 250 GB/month
- **Zones**: 1 storage zone
- **Replication**: Multi-region

### Paid Plan
- **Storage**: $0.005/GB (EU), $0.007/GB (US, Asia)
- **Bandwidth**: $0.003/GB (EU), $0.01/GB (Asia)
