## Wasabi

**Website**: https://wasabi.com
**Docs**: https://docs.wasabi.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/wasabi
**Category**: storage
**Subcategory**: object-storage

### Description
Hot cloud storage with no egress fees and no API request charges. Designed to be faster and cheaper than S3 for active storage. 1 GB permanent free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 1 GB permanent
- **Egress**: No charge
- **API requests**: No charge
- **Note**: 90-day minimum storage duration policy on paid plans

### Paid Plan
- **Storage**: $0.0059/GB
- **Egress**: Free
- **API**: Free
- **Note**: Min 90-day storage retention
