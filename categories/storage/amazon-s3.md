## Amazon S3

**Website**: https://aws.amazon.com/s3
**Docs**: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/s3
**GitHub**: https://github.com/aws
**Category**: storage
**Subcategory**: object-storage

### Description
Industry-standard object storage from AWS. 12-month free tier with 5 GB storage, 20K GET and 2K PUT requests. Unlimited scalability with 99.999999999% durability.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 5 GB standard
- **GET requests**: 20K/month
- **PUT requests**: 2K/month
- **Data transfer**: 15 GB out/month
- **Duration**: 12 months

### Paid Plan
- **Standard**: $0.023/GB storage
- **Infrequent Access**: $0.0125/GB
- **Egress**: $0.09/GB
