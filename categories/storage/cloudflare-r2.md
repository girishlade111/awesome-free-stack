## Cloudflare R2

**Website**: https://www.cloudflare.com/products/r2
**Docs**: https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2
**GitHub**: https://github.com/cloudflare
**Category**: storage
**Subcategory**: object-storage

### Description
S3-compatible object storage with zero egress fees. 10 GB free tier. Seamless integration with Cloudflare Workers, CDN, and the broader Cloudflare ecosystem.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **GET requests**: 1M/month
- **PUT requests**: 100K/month
- **Egress**: Zero (free)
- **Duration**: Permanent

### Paid Plan
- **Storage**: $0.015/GB
- **Egress**: $0.00/GB (free)
- **Class A ops**: $4.50/million
- **Class B ops**: $0.36/million
