## Backblaze B2

**Website**: https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage
**Docs**: https://www.backblaze.com/docs/cloud-storage
**GitHub**: https://github.com/backblaze-b2
**Category**: storage
**Subcategory**: object-storage

### Description
S3-compatible cloud storage with the cheapest egress among major providers. 10 GB free tier with 1,000 free downloads/day. Purpose-built for backup, CDN origin, and archive.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 10 GB
- **Downloads**: 1,000/day
- **Uploads**: 2,500/day
- **CDN egress**: 1 GB/day
- **Duration**: Permanent

### Paid Plan
- **Storage**: $0.006/GB
- **Egress**: $0.01/GB (cheapest major provider)
