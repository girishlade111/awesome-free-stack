## CrewAI

**Website**: https://crewai.com

### Description
Multi-agent orchestration framework for creating role-based AI teams. Agents with specific roles, goals, and backstories collaborate like a crew to complete complex tasks through delegation and coordination.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: MIT license, fully free self-hosted
- **Any LLM**: Works with OpenAI, Ollama, Anthropic, and local models
- **Unlimited usage**: No artificial limits on agents or tasks

### Paid Plan
- **CrewAI Enterprise**: Team collaboration features, custom pricing
