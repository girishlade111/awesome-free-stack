## LanceDB

**Website**: https://lancedb.com
**Docs**: https://lancedb.github.io/lancedb
**GitHub**: https://github.com/lancedb/lancedb
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: vector-databases

### Description
Developer-friendly, open-source vector database built on the Lance columnar format. Native multi-modal support with embedded zero-copy architecture and no server required — free forever.

### Free Tier
- **Self-hosted**: Completely free, unlimited vectors
- **LanceDB Cloud**: Free tier with limited storage
- **No server**: Embedded, zero-copy architecture

### Paid Plan
- **LanceDB Cloud**: Usage-based pricing
- **Self-hosted**: Free
