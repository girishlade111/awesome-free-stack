### Description
Adobe's commercial-safe generative AI platform for image creation and editing. Deeply integrated with Adobe Creative Cloud with legally safe training data suitable for commercial use.

### Free Tier
- **Generative credits**: 25 monthly generative credits on free plan
- **Generative fill**: Edit images with AI in Adobe Express
- **Text effects**: Free text-to-style and text effects
- **Commercial use**: Free tier images are safe for commercial projects

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Adobe Firefly Premium
- **Starting at**: $4.99/month (100 generative credits)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription
