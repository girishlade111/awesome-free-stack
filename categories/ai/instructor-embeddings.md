## Instructor Embeddings

**Website**: https://instructor-embedding.github.io
**Docs**: https://github.com/xlang-ai/instructor-embedding
**GitHub**: https://github.com/xlang-ai/instructor-embedding
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
Instructor Embeddings are task-specific embedding models that take instruction prefixes to generate customized embeddings for different use cases. Built on top of Sentence Transformers, they adapt to retrieval, clustering, classification, and similarity tasks.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free, MIT license
