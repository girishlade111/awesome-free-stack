### Description
Cloud platform for running open-source ML models via a simple API. Supports image generation, LLMs, video, audio, and more. Serverless GPU inference with per-second billing and a limited free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Credits**: Limited free tier with rate limits and free credits for experimentation (~$0.05 free usage)
- **Models**: Access to open-source community models
- **No monthly subscription**: Pay only for what you use

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Starting at**: $0.000225/second of GPU time
- **Billing**: Per-second usage-based
