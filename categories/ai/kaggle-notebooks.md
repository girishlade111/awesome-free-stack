### Description
Free cloud-based Jupyter notebook environment with GPU (NVIDIA T4 x2) and TPU acceleration. Access to 50K+ public datasets, community competitions, and pre-installed ML libraries — all free with a Kaggle account.

- 30 hours/week TPU v3-8
- 20GB notebook storage
- unlimited CPU usage
- access to 50K+ datasets
