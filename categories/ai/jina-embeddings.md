## Jina Embeddings

**Website**: https://jina.ai/embeddings
**Docs**: https://docs.jina.ai
**GitHub**: https://github.com/jina-ai
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
Jina AI offers open-source embedding models via cloud API with the Jina Embeddings platform. Provides state-of-the-art text and multimodal embeddings with a generous free tier of 1M tokens for development and prototyping.

### Free Tier
- **Tokens**: 1 million free tokens per month
- **Rate limit**: 100 requests per minute on free tier
- **Models**: jina-embeddings-v3 (text), jina-clip-v1 (multimodal)
- **No card required**: Free tier available without credit card

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go per token
- **Starting at**: $0.01/1K input tokens
- **Billing**: Usage-based
