## Lightning AI

**Website**: https://lightning.ai
**Docs**: https://lightning.ai/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/Lightning-AI
**Category**: ai

### Description
Platform for building, training, and deploying AI models with PyTorch Lightning. Provides cloud IDE with GPU access, multi-machine training, and one-click deployment. Free tier includes GPU compute credits.

### Free Tier
- 2 free GPU credits (~2 hours T4)
- unlimited CPU compute
- cloud IDE access
- 5GB persistent storage per project

### Paid Plan
- **Starting at: $50
- **Billing**: subscription
