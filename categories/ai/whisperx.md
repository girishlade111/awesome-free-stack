### Description
Accelerated Whisper inference with word-level timestamps, speaker diarization, and voice activity detection. Uses CTranslate2 for 70x faster than real-time transcription on GPU with batch processing.

### Free Tier
- **Pricing**: Completely free and open source
- **Models**: Supports large-v2, large-v3, distilled variants
- **Features**: Word-level timestamps, speaker diarization, alignment
- **Hardware**: CPU inference, GPU (CUDA) recommended
