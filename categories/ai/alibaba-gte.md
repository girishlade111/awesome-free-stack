## Alibaba GTE

**Website**: https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-NLP/gte-Qwen2-7B-instruct
**Docs**: https://huggingface.co/Alibaba-NLP
**GitHub**: https://github.com/AlibabaResearch/gte
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
Alibaba's GTE (General Text Embeddings) models are state-of-the-art multilingual embedding models built on the Qwen architecture. GTE-Qwen2 achieves top scores on MTEB benchmarks with strong support for Chinese and English text retrieval.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free, Apache 2.0 license
- **Models**: GTE-small, GTE-base, GTE-large, GTE-Qwen2
- **Usage**: Unlimited self-hosted usage
- **No card required**: Free to download from HuggingFace
