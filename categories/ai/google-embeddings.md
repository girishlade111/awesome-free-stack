## Google Embeddings

**Website**: https://ai.google.dev
**Docs**: https://ai.google.dev/docs/embeddings
**GitHub**: https://github.com/google-gemini
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
Google's text embedding models via the Gemini API, including text-embedding-004 and multimodal embeddings. Offers a generous free tier with 1M token context window and excellent multilingual support for semantic search and classification.

### Free Tier
- **Rate limit**: 60 requests per minute, 1,500 requests per day
- **Models**: text-embedding-004 (free tier), multimodal embeddings
- **No credit card**: Sign up with Google account only
- **Context window**: 1M tokens for Gemini models

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Starting at**: $0.0001/1K input characters (embedding)
- **Billing**: Usage-based
