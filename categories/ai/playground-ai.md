### Description
Free image generation platform offering access to Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, and custom models through a user-friendly web interface. Provides daily free credits for prompt-based image creation.

### Free Tier
- **Daily credits**: 50 free image generations per day
- **Models**: Access to Playground v2, SDXL, and DALL-E 3
- **Image editing**: Built-in inpainting and outpainting tools
- **No card required**: Sign up with Google or Discord

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Playground AI Pro
- **Starting at**: $15/month (1,000 credits)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription
