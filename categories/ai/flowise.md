## Flowise

**Website**: https://flowiseai.com
**Docs**: https://docs.flowiseai.com

**Category**: ai

### Description
Low-code, drag-and-drop LLM application builder for creating AI agents, chatbots, and RAG pipelines visually. No coding required to build complex AI workflows with a free and open-source tier.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Apache 2.0, fully free self-hosted
- **Docker deploy**: One-command deployment
- **50+ nodes**: Built-in tools, models, and integrations

### Paid Plan
- **Flowise Cloud**: Managed hosting with higher limits
