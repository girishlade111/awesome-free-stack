## Astra DB

**Website**: https://astra.datastax.com
**Docs**: https://docs.datastax.com/en/astra-serverless
**GitHub**: https://github.com/datastax
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: vector-databases

### Description
Serverless vector database built on Apache Cassandra by DataStax. Offers a generous 80GB free tier with built-in integrations for LangChain, LlamaIndex, and OpenAI.

### Free Tier
- **Storage**: 80GB data free
- **Reads**: 25 million monthly reads free
- **Writes**: 5 million monthly writes free
- **Serverless**: Auto-scaling, pay-per-request

### Paid Plan
- **Pay-as-you-go**: Usage-based beyond free tier
