## LangSmith Prompt Hub

**Website**: https://smith.langchain.com
**Docs**: https://docs.smith.langchain.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/langchain-ai/langsmith
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: prompt-tools

### Description
A collaborative prompt management and versioning platform integrated with the LangChain ecosystem. Enables teams to version, test, evaluate, and deploy prompts with integrated tracing and feedback collection.

### Free Tier
- **Prompts**: Unlimited prompt creation with version history
- **Traces**: 5,000 traces per month for prompt evaluation
- **Users**: 1 user on free tier
- **Retention**: 7-day trace retention

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Team
- **Starting at**: $99/month
- **Billing**: subscription
