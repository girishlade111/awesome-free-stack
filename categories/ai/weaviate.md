## Weaviate

**Website**: https://weaviate.io
**Docs**: https://weaviate.io/developers/weaviate
**GitHub**: https://github.com/weaviate/weaviate
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: vector-databases

### Description
Open-source vector database with built-in vectorization modules, hybrid search (vector + keyword), and graph-like data model. Combines structured and unstructured data with a free cloud sandbox free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Cloud sandbox**: 1 cluster, up to 1GB storage, free
- **Self-hosted**: Unlimited, completely free
- **Built-in modules**: OpenAI, Cohere, Hugging Face vectorizers

### Paid Plan
- **Weaviate Cloud Standard**: Usage-based dedicated clusters
- **Self-hosted**: Free
