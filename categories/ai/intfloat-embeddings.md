## intfloat Embeddings

**Website**: https://huggingface.co/intfloat
**Docs**: https://huggingface.co/intfloat
**GitHub**: https://github.com/intfloat
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
The intfloat collection includes E5 and multilingual E5 embedding models that consistently rank at the top of MTEB leaderboards. E5 (EmbEddings from bidirEctional Encoder rEpresentations) delivers strong zero-shot performance for retrieval and semantic similarity tasks.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free, MIT license
- **Models**: e5-small-v2, e5-base-v2, e5-large-v2, multilingual-e5
- **Usage**: Unlimited self-hosted usage
- **No card required**: Free to download from HuggingFace
