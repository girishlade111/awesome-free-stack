### Description
The most popular framework for building LLM-powered applications. Provides a modular ecosystem for chains, agents, tools, memory, and RAG integration with 700+ built-in integrations — all free and open source.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: MIT license, fully free self-hosted
- **LangSmith**: 5,000 traces/month free
- **No limits**: Unlimited agents, chains, and tools when self-hosted

### Paid Plan
- **LangSmith Enterprise**: Custom pricing for advanced observability
