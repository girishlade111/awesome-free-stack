## ChunkViz

**Website**: https://chunkviz.upstash.app
**Docs**: https://github.com/upstash/chunkviz
**GitHub**: https://github.com/upstash/chunkviz
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: rag

### Description
Open-source visualizer for text chunking strategies in RAG pipelines. Helps developers compare different chunking methods (recursive, semantic, fixed-size) and see how documents get split before embedding. Essential debugging tool for RAG quality.
