**Website**: https://www.baai.ac.cn

### Description
BAAI's BGE (BAAI General Embedding) models are among the most popular open-source embedding models on HuggingFace. The BGE series delivers top-tier performance on MTEB benchmarks and supports multiple languages with various model sizes.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free, MIT license
- **Models**: bge-small-en, bge-base-en, bge-large-en, bge-m3
- **Usage**: Unlimited self-hosted usage
- **No card required**: Download from HuggingFace for free
