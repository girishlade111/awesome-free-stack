### Description
Open-source ML platform for Kubernetes that orchestrates the entire ML workflow — from pipeline creation and experiment tracking to model serving and monitoring. Industry standard for enterprise MLOps on K8s.

### Free Tier
- Completely free under Apache 2.0 license. Self-hosted on any Kubernetes cluster with unlimited pipelines
- notebooks
- and model serving.

### Paid Plan
- **Model: No paid version; fully open source. Cloud provider K8s services have separate pricing.
- **Billing**: usage-based
