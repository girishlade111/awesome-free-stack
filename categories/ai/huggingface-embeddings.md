## Hugging Face Embeddings

**Website**: https://huggingface.co
**Docs**: https://huggingface.co/docs/api-inference
**GitHub**: https://github.com/huggingface
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
Access thousands of open-source embedding models on Hugging Face via the free Inference API. Supports text, image, and multimodal embedding models from BAAI, Jina, Nomic, Sentence Transformers, and more with a simple unified API.

### Free Tier
- **Tokens**: 30,000 free input tokens per month
- **Models**: 150,000+ models including BGE, Instructor, E5, CLIP
- **Rate limits**: Rate-limited for serverless inference endpoints
- **No card required**: Free tier without credit card

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Dedicated endpoints or Pro account
- **Starting at**: $9/month (Pro) or $0.06/hour (CPU endpoint)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription or usage-based
