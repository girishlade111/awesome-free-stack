## Hugging Face Spaces

**Website**: https://huggingface.co/spaces
**Docs**: https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces
**GitHub**: https://github.com/huggingface
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: ml-platforms

### Description
Free hosting platform for ML demo apps using Gradio, Streamlit, or Docker. Deploy interactive AI demos with CPU/GPU support, custom domains, and seamless Hugging Face Hub integration with a generous free tier.

### Free Tier
- Unlimited CPU spaces
- 2 GPU spaces (T4 small)
- 30GB storage
- community tier includes 500 monthly active users

### Paid Plan
- **Starting at: $9
- **Billing**: subscription
