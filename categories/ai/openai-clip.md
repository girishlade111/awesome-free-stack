## OpenAI CLIP

**Website**: https://openai.com/research/clip
**Docs**: https://huggingface.co/openai/clip-vit-base-patch32
**GitHub**: https://github.com/openai/CLIP
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: embeddings

### Description
OpenAI's CLIP (Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training) model generates joint embeddings for text and images. Enables zero-shot image classification, image-text similarity search, and multimodal retrieval — widely used in AI art and search applications.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free, MIT license
- **Models**: CLIP-ViT-B/32, CLIP-ViT-L/14, and variants
- **Usage**: Unlimited local usage
- **No card required**: Free to download and use
