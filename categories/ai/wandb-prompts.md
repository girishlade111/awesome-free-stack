### Description
A prompt engineering and LLM observability module within the Weights & Biases ML platform. Tracks prompts, model responses, tokens, and costs with rich visualizations, versioning, and team collaboration.

### Free Tier
- **Dashboard**: Unlimited personal dashboard
- **Logging**: Log prompt traces and model responses
- **Storage**: 100 GB of artifacts storage
- **Reports**: Unlimited shared reports
