## Deepgram

**Website**: https://deepgram.com
**Docs**: https://developers.deepgram.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/deepgram
**Category**: ai

### Description
Real-time speech-to-text API powered by the Nova-2 model, the fastest and most accurate ASR engine. Supports 30+ languages with punctuation, diarization, and custom vocabulary.

### Free Tier
- **Hours**: $200 credit ($200 free initial credit)
- **Text hours**: 5 hours per month free for pre-recorded audio
- **Real-time**: 5 hours per month free for streaming
- **Rate limit**: Pay-as-you-go credit model

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Starting at**: $0.0043/min (Nova-2 pre-recorded)
- **Billing**: Usage-based per audio minute
