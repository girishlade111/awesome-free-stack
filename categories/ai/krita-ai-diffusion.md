## Krita AI Diffusion

**Website**: https://github.com/Acly/krita-ai-diffusion

### Description
Open-source plugin that integrates Stable Diffusion directly into Krita, the professional digital painting application. Enables in-context generation, inpainting, outpainting, and live painting with AI assistance.

### Free Tier
- **Open source**: Fully free under GPL-3.0 license
- **Local inference**: Runs on your GPU with no usage limits
- **Krita integration**: Seamless layer-based AI painting workflow
- **Models**: Supports SD 1.5, SDXL, and Flux via local or API backends
