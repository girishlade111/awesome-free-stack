## RVC (Retrieval-based Voice Conversion)

**Website**: https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI
**Docs**: https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI/blob/main/docs/README.en.md
**GitHub**: https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: speech

### Description
Open-source real-time voice conversion tool using retrieval-based methods with a WebUI. Extracts speaker embeddings using HuBERT, trains lightweight vocoders, and converts voice with minimal latency.

### Free Tier
- **Pricing**: Completely free and open source
- **Features**: Real-time voice conversion, speaker embedding training
- **Training**: Train custom voice models on 10-60 minutes of audio
- **Hardware**: GPU (CUDA) required for training; CPU real-time inference possible
