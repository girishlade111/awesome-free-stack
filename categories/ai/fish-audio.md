**Website**: https://fish.audio

### Description
Speech synthesis and voice cloning API with fast inference and high-quality output. Offers text-to-speech, voice cloning from short audio samples, and a growing marketplace of community voices.

### Free Tier
- **Credits**: $1 free credit on signup
- **Characters**: 60,000 characters per month free
- **Voices**: Access to 300+ community voices
- **Features**: TTS API, voice cloning, voice marketplace

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Starting at**: $0.0015/1K characters (standard voices)
- **Billing**: Usage-based per character
