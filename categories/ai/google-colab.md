## Google Colab

**Website**: https://colab.research.google.com
**Docs**: https://colab.research.google.com/notebooks
**GitHub**: https://github.com/googlecolab
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: ml-platforms

### Description
Free Jupyter notebook environment with GPU and TPU acceleration for machine learning experimentation. No setup required — runs entirely in the browser with pre-installed ML libraries and seamless Google Drive integration.

### Free Tier
- **GPU**: Free T4 GPU (up to 12 hours per session)
- **TPU**: Free TPU v2-8 access (limited availability)
- **Compute**: 12GB system RAM, 78GB disk
- **Session limits**: 12-hour max session duration

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Colab Pro / Pro+
- **Starting at**: $9.99/month (Pro), $49.99/month (Pro+)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription
