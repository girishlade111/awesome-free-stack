## Microsoft Azure Speech

**Website**: https://azure.microsoft.com/products/ai-services/ai-speech
**Docs**: https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-services/speech-service
**GitHub**: https://github.com/Azure-Samples
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: speech

### Description
Microsoft's cloud speech platform offering both speech-to-text and text-to-speech via a unified Speech SDK. Supports real-time translation, custom voice models, and speaker recognition.

### Free Tier
- **STT audio**: 5 hours per month free (standard model)
- **TTS characters**: 5 million characters per month free (neural voices)
- **Custom voice**: 1 endpoint model free
- **Features**: Real-time API, batch transcription, intent recognition

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Starting at**: $0.006/min (STT standard), $0.015/1M chars (TTS neural)
- **Billing**: Usage-based per audio minute or character
