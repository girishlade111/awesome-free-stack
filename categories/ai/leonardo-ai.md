### Description
Feature-rich AI image generation platform with a generous free tier and integrated training tools. Offers custom model training, image generation, editing, and a community gallery with millions of creations.

### Free Tier
- **Daily tokens**: 150 free tokens per day (roughly 30-50 images)
- **Custom models**: Train your own AI models for free
- **Style presets**: 100+ community style presets
- **Image dimensions**: Up to 1536x1536 on free tier

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Leonardo Pro
- **Starting at**: $10/month (2,000 tokens)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription
