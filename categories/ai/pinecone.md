## Pinecone

**Website**: https://www.pinecone.io
**Docs**: https://docs.pinecone.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/pinecone-io
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: vector-databases

### Description
Fully managed vector database purpose-built for production AI applications. Offers serverless and pod-based indexes with advanced filtering, hybrid search, and high throughput with a 1M vector free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Vectors**: 1 million vectors free (pod-based)
- **Serverless**: Free tier with limited throughput
- **Zero ops**: Automatic scaling and replication

### Paid Plan
- **Pod-based**: From $0.075/pod-hour
- **Serverless**: $0.70/M vectors stored + queries
- **Billing**: Usage-based
