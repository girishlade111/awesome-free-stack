## Sentence Transformers

**Website**: https://sbert.net
**Docs**: https://sbert.net/docs

**Category**: ai

### Description
The most popular open-source Python library for generating sentence, text, and image embeddings. Provides thousands of pre-trained transformer models for semantic search, clustering, and paraphrase mining with easy-to-use APIs.
