## Bark

**Website**: https://huggingface.co/suno/bark
**Docs**: https://github.com/suno-ai/bark
**GitHub**: https://github.com/suno-ai/bark
**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: speech

### Description
Suno's transformer-based text-to-speech model capable of generating realistic speech with emotion, pitch, rhythm, and non-verbal sounds like laughter and sighs. Supports 25+ languages and voice cloning.

### Free Tier
- **Pricing**: Completely free and open source
- **Models**: Full model weights available on Hugging Face
- **Features**: Emotional TTS, non-verbal sounds, voice cloning
- **Hardware**: GPU (CUDA) recommended; CPU inference possible

### Paid Plan
None — fully open-source
