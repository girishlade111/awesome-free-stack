## pgvector

**Website**: https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector

**Category**: ai
**Subcategory**: vector-databases

### Description
Open-source vector similarity search extension for PostgreSQL. The most widely deployed vector database — available on every major Postgres provider (Supabase, Neon, RDS) with a 500MB free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Supabase**: 500MB PostgreSQL free (includes pgvector)
- **Neon**: 0.5GB serverless Postgres free
- **Self-hosted**: Unlimited, completely free
- **Full ACID**: PostgreSQL transactional guarantees

### Paid Plan
- Free with PostgreSQL; cloud providers have separate pricing for larger databases.
