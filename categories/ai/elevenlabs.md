### Description
AI voice synthesis platform offering ultra-realistic text-to-speech, voice cloning, and dubbing. Industry-leading voice quality with multilingual support and emotion control.

### Free Tier
- **Characters**: 10,000 characters per month
- **Voices**: Access to 100+ built-in voices
- **Features**: Text-to-speech API, speech-to-speech, voice library
- **Rate limit**: 25,000 characters per month on API (free tier)

### Paid Plan
- **Model**: Pay-as-you-go / subscription
- **Starting at**: $5/month (Starter)
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription with character quotas
