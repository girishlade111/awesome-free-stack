### Description
AI-powered code snippet manager and productivity tool. Automatically saves snippets from your IDE, browser, and clipboard with AI-generated titles and descriptions. Features on-device AI for offline use and context retrieval.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: unlimited local snippets, AI enrichment, on-device AI, and 3 cloud-connected devices
- All core features included.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $20/month
- Pro from $20/month for unlimited cloud snippets, team sharing, and advanced AI models.
