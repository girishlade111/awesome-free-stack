## Composer

**Website**: https://getcomposer.org
**Docs**: https://getcomposer.org/doc/
**GitHub**: https://github.com/composer/composer
**Category**: devtools
**Subcategory**: package-managers

### Description
PHP dependency manager with Packagist registry. Manages libraries, autoloading, and platform requirements. Uses composer.json and composer.lock for deterministic dependency resolution across environments.

### Free Tier
- Completely free open-source under MIT license
- Packagist registry free for public and private packages.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $10/month
- Private Packagist from $10/month for private package hosting and team features.
