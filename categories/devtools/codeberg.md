## Codeberg

**Website**: https://codeberg.org
**Docs**: https://docs.codeberg.org
**Category**: devtools
**Subcategory**: version-control

### Description
Community-driven Git hosting platform run by the Codeberg non-profit. Based on Forgejo (Gitea fork). Free for all open source projects worldwide. Privacy-focused with no advertising or tracking.

### Free Tier
- Completely free for open source projects and personal use
- Unlimited public and private repositories
- No limits on collaborators.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: N/A
- Donation-supported platform. No paid tiers — entirely community-funded and free for all users.
