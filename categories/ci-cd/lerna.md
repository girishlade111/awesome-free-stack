## Lerna

**Website**: https://lerna.js.org
**Docs**: https://lerna.js.org/docs/introduction
**GitHub**: https://github.com/lerna/lerna
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: build-automation

### Description
Monorepo management tool for JavaScript/TypeScript projects. Optimizes workflows around managing multi-package repositories with git, npm/yarn/pnpm.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. No usage limits. Works with any npm/yarn/pnpm registry.
