### Description
Simple, agentless IT automation platform for configuration management, application deployment, and orchestration. YAML-based playbooks, push-based architecture, and massive module ecosystem. Red Hat backed.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source (AWX is free GUI). Ansible Automation Platform has a 60-day trial. Community documentation, collections, and modules are free.
