## Cloudsmith

**Website**: https://cloudsmith.io
**Docs**: https://help.cloudsmith.io/docs

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: artifact-hosting

### Description
Cloud-native artifact management platform supporting 30+ package formats. Host and proxy containers, packages, and binaries with built-in vulnerability scanning.

### Free Tier
1 GB storage, 1 GB transfer/month. Unlimited public repositories. 5 team members. Vulnerability scanning included.

### Paid Plan
Pro — from /month: 50 GB storage, 50 GB transfer, unlimited members
