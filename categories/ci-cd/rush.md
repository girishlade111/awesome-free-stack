## Rush

**Website**: https://rushjs.io
**Docs**: https://rushjs.io/pages/intro/get_started
**GitHub**: https://github.com/microsoft/rushstack
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: build-automation

### Description
Microsoft's scalable monorepo manager for large teams. Intelligent orchestration of npm/pnpm/yarn workspaces with parallel builds, incremental builds, and phased builds.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Unlimited projects. Works with any package manager.
