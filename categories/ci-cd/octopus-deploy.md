## Octopus Deploy

**Website**: https://octopus.com
**Docs**: https://octopus.com/docs

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: deployment-automation

### Description
Release management and deployment automation for .NET and cloud-native applications. Variable management, project templates, lifecycle management, and built-in rollbacks. Free for up to 10 targets.

### Free Tier
Free Community tier: 10 deployment targets, unlimited projects, all features. Self-hosted also available.

### Paid Plan
Team — from /month: 100 deployment targets, 50 users, support
