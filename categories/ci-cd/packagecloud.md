## PackageCloud

**Website**: https://packagecloud.io
**Docs**: https://docs.packagecloud.io

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: artifact-hosting

### Description
Hosted package repository for Debian, RPM, RubyGems, Python, Node.js, Java, and more. Private and public repos with team collaboration and CI/CD integration.

### Free Tier
1 private repository, unlimited public repositories. 200 MB storage. 1 GB transfer/month. 2 team members.

### Paid Plan
Personal — /month: 10GB storage, 10GB transfer, 5 users. Team — /month
