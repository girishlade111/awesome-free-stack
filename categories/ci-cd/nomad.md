## Nomad

**Website**: https://www.nomadproject.io
**Docs**: https://developer.hashicorp.com/nomad/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/hashicorp/nomad
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: deployment-automation

### Description
HashiCorp's flexible workload orchestrator for containers, VMs, and standalone applications. Single binary, multi-region federation, and native service discovery with Consul. Simpler alternative to Kubernetes for many workloads.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Self-hosted. Supports Docker, Podman, Java, raw exec, and QEMU drivers.

### Paid Plan
Nomad Enterprise — from /seat/month: governance, multi-tenancy, federation, priority support
