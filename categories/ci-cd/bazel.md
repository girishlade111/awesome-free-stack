### Description
Google's fast, scalable, multi-language build system. Correct incremental builds with fine-grained dependency tracking, distributed caching, and remote execution. Supports Java, C++, Python, Go, Android, iOS, and more.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Self-hosted. Unlimited builds.
