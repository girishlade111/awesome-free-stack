## Woodpecker CI

**Website**: https://woodpecker-ci.org
**Docs**: https://woodpecker-ci.org/docs/intro
**GitHub**: https://github.com/woodpecker-ci/woodpecker
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: pipelines

### Description
Community fork of Drone CI focused on simplicity and reliability. YAML-based pipeline configuration with Docker and Kubernetes runners. Supports GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, and Bitbucket repositories. Completely open-source and self-hostable.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Self-hosted, unlimited builds, unlimited repos, unlimited users.
