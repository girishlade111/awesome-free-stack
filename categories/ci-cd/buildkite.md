## Buildkite

**Website**: https://buildkite.com
**Docs**: https://buildkite.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/buildkite
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: pipelines

### Description
Hybrid CI/CD platform that runs your builds on your own infrastructure with a centralized web UI. Connect your own agents (any OS) for unlimited parallelism and full control over your build environment.

### Free Tier
- **Users**: 1
- **Agents**: 5
- **Pipelines**: Unlimited
- **Builds**: Unlimited
- **Artifacts**: 30 day retention
- **History**: 90 days

### Paid Plan
- **Teams**: $15/user/month
- **Features**: Unlimited agents, SAML SSO, priority support, audit log
