### Description
Netflix-inspired multi-cloud continuous delivery platform. Advanced deployment strategies (blue/green, canary, rolling), pipeline templates, and managed infrastructure. Supports AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, and more.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Self-hosted. Supports all major cloud providers and Kubernetes.
