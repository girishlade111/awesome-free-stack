## MyGet

**Website**: https://www.myget.org
**Docs**: https://docs.myget.org

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: artifact-hosting

### Description
Hosted package registry supporting NuGet, npm, Maven, Bower, VSIX, and more. CI/CD integration with GitHub, Azure DevOps, and Bitbucket.

### Free Tier
1 private feed, unlimited public feeds. 500 MB storage. 2 team members. 1 upstream source.

### Paid Plan
Pro — from /month: 10 private feeds, 5 GB storage, 25 team members
