## Bitbucket Pipelines

**Website**: https://bitbucket.org/product/features/pipelines
**Docs**: https://support.atlassian.com/bitbucket-cloud/docs/get-started-with-bitbucket-pipelines

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: pipelines

### Description
Atlassian's integrated CI/CD for Bitbucket repositories. YAML-based configuration, Docker-native builds, deploy to any cloud.

### Free Tier
50 build minutes/month. 1 parallel step. 1 GB artifact storage. Free for up to 5 users in Bitbucket Cloud free plan.

### Paid Plan
Standard — from $3/user/month: 2,500 min, 10 parallel steps. Premium: $6/user/month
