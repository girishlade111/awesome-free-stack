## Just

**Website**: https://just.systems
**Docs**: https://just.systems/man/en
**GitHub**: https://github.com/casey/just
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: build-automation

### Description
Lightweight command runner alternative to Make. Recipes with parameters, dependencies, and cross-platform shell commands. Single-binary, no dependencies, written in Rust.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Single binary, works on all platforms.
