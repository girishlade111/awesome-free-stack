## Cucumber

**Website**: https://cucumber.io
**Docs**: https://cucumber.io/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/cucumber
**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: testing-automation

### Description
BDD (Behavior-Driven Development) testing framework supporting Gherkin syntax. Write human-readable test scenarios that execute as automated tests.

### Free Tier
Completely free and open-source. Cucumber Studio (collaboration) has a free tier: 5 users, 5 repositories.

### Paid Plan
Cucumber Studio — from /month for 10 users, unlimited repos
