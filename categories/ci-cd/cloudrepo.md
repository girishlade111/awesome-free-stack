## CloudRepo

**Website**: https://cloudrepo.io
**Docs**: https://docs.cloudrepo.io

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: artifact-hosting

### Description
Hosted private Maven and PyPI repository. Secure artifact storage with access controls, team management, and audit logging. No infrastructure to manage.

### Free Tier
250 MB storage. 1 GB bandwidth/month. Unlimited repos. 3 users. Maven and PyPI support.

### Paid Plan
Team — from /month: 5 GB storage, 25 GB bandwidth, 25 users
