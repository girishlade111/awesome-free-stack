## Codemagic

**Website**: https://codemagic.io
**Docs**: https://docs.codemagic.io

**Category**: ci-cd
**Subcategory**: pipelines

### Description
CI/CD platform specialized for Flutter and mobile app development. Automates building, testing, and publishing to app stores. Pre-configured workflows for Flutter, React Native, and native Android/iOS.

### Free Tier
500 build minutes/month. Unlimited projects. 1 concurrent build. Builds for open-source projects get 1,200 minutes/month.

### Paid Plan
Personal — from $18/month: 2,500 min, 2 concurrent builds. Team: $48/month: 5,000 min, 5 concurrent builds.
