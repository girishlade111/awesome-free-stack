## Credits & Benefits

- **CRM**: 90% off all Hubs (Marketing, Sales, Service, Operations, CMS)
- **Onboarding**: Free dedicated onboarding specialist
- **Academy**: HubSpot Academy access
- **Community**: Startup-only community events
- **API**: Full API access for integrations
- **Support**: Priority customer support

## Pros

- 90% discount on all HubSpot products
- Free onboarding specialist
- Comprehensive CRM + marketing + sales stack
- HubSpot Academy for team training
- Scales from startup to enterprise

## Cons

- Only 90% off (not free)
- Limited to 1 year
- Credit card required
- Can be expensive at full price after discount

## Key Features

- CRM with contact and deal management
- Marketing Hub (email, automation, analytics)
- Sales Hub (sequences, meetings, quotes)
- Service Hub (tickets, knowledge base)
- CMS Hub (website builder)
- Operations Hub (data sync, automation)

## Alternatives

- Salesforce for Startups — similar program
- Pipedrive — simpler, cheaper CRM
- Zoho CRM — free tier available
