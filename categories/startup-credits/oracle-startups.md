- **OCI Credits**: Up to $10,000 in cloud credits (2 years)
- **Always Free**: Can stack with OCI Always Free tier

- **AI**: OCI AI and ML services
- **Kubernetes**: OKE managed Kubernetes
- **Support**: OCI technical support included

## Pros

- Highest raw credit value ($10K)
- Can stack with always-free tier
- Powerful autonomous database
- ARM Ampere instances (high performance/price)
- 2 year validity

## Cons

- Smaller ecosystem than AWS/GCP/Azure
- Less startup community support
- Documentation and DX not as refined
- Oracle's reputation can be a concern
