## Credits & Benefits

- **Processing Credits**: $500 in Stripe fee credits
- **Incorporation**: US Delaware C-Corp formation
- **Bank Account**: US business bank account setup
- **Tax**: Initial registered agent and EIN
- **Partners**: 100+ partner offers (AWS, Google, Notion, etc.)
- **Legal**: Standard legal documents

## Pros

- Complete US incorporation package
- $500 in free payment processing
- 100+ partner offers included
- Easy to use and well-documented
- Standard legal docs for fundraising

## Cons

- $500 fee for incorporation ($0 with some partners)
- US-focused (Delaware C-Corp only)
- May need additional legal help for complex cases
- Not suitable for non-US founders (complex tax)

## Key Features

- Delaware C-Corp incorporation
- Stripe payment processing ($500 credits)

- EIN and registered agent
- 100+ partner discounts
- Standard fundraising documents
