Free cloud credits, SaaS programs, incubator access, and founder perks for early-stage startups.

## Selection Guide

- **Best overall cloud**: AWS Activate — most comprehensive, largest ecosystem
- **Best AI credits**: Microsoft Founders Hub — Azure + OpenAI in one program
- **Best for GPU/hardware startups**: NVIDIA Inception — up to $100K in GPU credits
- **Best for incorporation**: Stripe Atlas — $500 in processing + Delaware incorporation
- **Best communication credits**: Twilio for Startups — $1K across all Twilio APIs
- **Best identity/security**: Auth0 for Startups — 10K free users, no expiry
- **Best CRM discount**: HubSpot for Startups — 90% off for 1 year
- **Best startup education**: Y Combinator Startup School — free + $100K partner credits
- **Best for bootstrapped startups**: DigitalOcean Hatch — simple pricing, $5K credits
- **Best for AI-native startups**: OpenAI for Startups — free API credits + early access

> **Note**: Most programs require a valid startup (not individual developers) and may involve an application process. Credit card is often required even for free credits to prevent abuse. Many programs can be stacked — e.g., use AWS Activate + Stripe Atlas + YC Startup School simultaneously. NVIDIA Inception and Auth0 for Startups are among the few programs that don't require a credit card.
