## Tips

- **Apply without an MVP** — Most accelerators accept ideas (especially YC and EF)
- **Stack accelerator + direct credits** — Accelerator perks are additive to direct startup programs
- **No-equity options exist** — Google, Microsoft, and Oracle have self-serve programs
- **NVIDIA Inception** — Best for AI startups needing GPU compute
- **Deadlines matter** — YC and Techstars have fixed batches; apply early
- **Network effect** — Accelerator alumni network is worth more than the funding
