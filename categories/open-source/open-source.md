# Open-Source Alternatives

Curated collection of open-source, self-hostable alternatives to popular paid tools — giving you full control over your data and no ongoing subscription costs.

## Quick Comparison

| Tool | Paid Alternative | Free Limit | Self-Host | Card? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ollama** | ChatGPT / Claude | 100% free (unlimited local LLMs) | ✅ | No |
| **Open WebUI** | ChatGPT | 100% free (ChatGPT-like UI for local LLMs) | ✅ | No |
| **AppFlowy** | Notion | 100% free (docs, DB, kanban, AI) | ✅ | No |
| **Outline** | Notion / Confluence | Self-hosted free; Cloud: 1 team free | ✅ | No |
| **AFFiNE** | Notion + Miro | 100% free (docs + whiteboards) | ✅ | No |
| **PocketBase** | Firebase | 100% free (single binary backend) | ✅ | No |
| **Kamal** | Vercel / K8s | 100% free (Docker deploy tool) | ✅ | No |
| **Cal.com** | Calendly | Self-hosted free; Cloud: 1 user free | ✅ | No |
| **Documenso** | DocuSign | Self-hosted free; Cloud: 2 team members | ✅ | No |
| **NocoDB** | Airtable | Self-hosted free; Cloud: 50K rows | ✅ | No |
