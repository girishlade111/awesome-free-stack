## Free Tier Details

- **Documents**: Unlimited
- **Team Members**: 2 (cloud), unlimited (self-hosted)
- **Templates**: Included
- **Audit Trail**: Included
- **Branding**: Basic customization
- **API**: Included

## Key Features

- Drag-and-drop signature fields
- Document templates
- Email reminders and notifications
- Audit trail and certificate of completion
- Team workflows and approvals
- API and webhooks
- Self-hosted (Docker)
- Custom branding

## Alternatives To

- DocuSign — Documenso is the leading open-source alternative
