### Description
Serverless platform for deploying full-stack apps, APIs, and databases globally. Supports Docker, Git-based deployment, and native auto-scaling across multiple regions with a free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Apps**: 1 web app + 1 worker
- **RAM**: 512 MB
- **Bandwidth**: 100 GB/month
- **Custom domains**: Supported with auto SSL

### Paid Plan
- **Starter**: $5/month
- **Upgrades**: 2 GB RAM, 4 apps
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription
