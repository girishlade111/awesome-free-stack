## Vercel

**Website**: https://vercel.com
**Docs**: https://vercel.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/vercel
**Category**: hosting

### Description
Frontend deployment platform with global edge network. Supports Next.js, React, Svelte, and all major frameworks. Includes serverless functions, edge config, and analytics with a generous free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Static sites**: Unlimited
- **Bandwidth**: 100 GB/month
- **Build minutes**: 6,000 minutes/month
- **Concurrent builds**: 1
- **Serverless functions**: 100 invocations/day
- **Edge functions**: 10 invocations/day

### Paid Plan
- **Pro**: $20/month per member
- **Upgrades**: Unlimited builds, 1 TB bandwidth, 5 concurrent builds, team features
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription per member
