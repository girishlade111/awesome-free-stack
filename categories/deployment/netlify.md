**Website**: https://netlify.com
**Docs**: https://docs.netlify.com

### Description
Pioneering JAMstack deployment platform with global CDN, serverless functions, forms, identity, and split testing. Git-based CI/CD with deploy previews and a generous free tier.

### Free Tier
- **Bandwidth**: 100 GB/month
- **Build minutes**: 300 minutes/month
- **Serverless functions**: 125K invocations/month
- **Form submissions**: 100/month
- **Active users**: 1K (Identity)

### Paid Plan
- **Pro**: $19/month per member
- **Upgrades**: Unlimited builds, 1 TB bandwidth, priority support
- **Billing**: Monthly subscription per member
