## Fly.io

**Website**: https://fly.io
**Docs**: https://fly.io/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/superfly
**Category**: deployment
**Subcategory**: containers

### Description
Docker container hosting platform that runs your app on the edge. Deploy containers close to users with global Anycast network, auto-scaling, and free tier with 3 shared VMs.

### Free Tier
- **VMs**: 3 shared VMs (256MB RAM each)
- **Storage**: 3 GB
- **Bandwidth**: 160 GB/month

### Paid Plan
- **Pricing**: Pay-as-you-go
- **RAM**: $0.0017/GB/hour
- **CPU**: $0.0004/CPU/hour
- **Billing**: Usage-based
