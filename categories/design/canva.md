### Description
Free graphic design platform for non-designers. Drag-and-drop editor with thousands of templates for social media, presentations, posters, videos, and more. Built-in stock photos, fonts, and design assets.

### Free Tier
- **Templates**: 250,000+ free
- **Photos**: 1M+ free stock photos
- **Storage**: 5GB cloud
- **Design types**: 100+ categories

### Paid Plan
- **Pro**: $12.99/month
- **Features**: 100M+ premium assets, 100GB storage, brand kits, background removal, scheduling
