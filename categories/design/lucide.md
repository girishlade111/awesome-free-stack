## Lucide

**Website**: https://lucide.dev
**Docs**: https://lucide.dev/guide
**GitHub**: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide
**Category**: design
**Subcategory**: icons

### Description
Beautiful open-source icon library — the successor to Feather Icons. 1,500+ consistent SVG icons with clean, minimal designs. Available as React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, and vanilla SVG packages with tree-shaking.

### Free Tier
- **Icons**: 1,500+ free SVG icons
- **Format**: React, Vue, Svelte, Solid, Angular, Preact, Figma, SVG
- **License**: MIT — free for commercial use
- **Features**: Tree-shakeable, customizable stroke width, size, and colors

### Paid Plan
- **Price**: No paid version
- **Model**: Completely free and open-source
