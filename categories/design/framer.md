### Description
Professional prototyping and web design tool with built-in publishing. Design interactive prototypes with components, variants, and smart animate. Publish directly as websites with customizable CMS and SEO.

### Free Tier
- **Sites**: 2 published sites
- **Domain**: framer.app subdomain
- **CMS**: 1 collection
- **Forms**: 100 submissions/month
- **Templates**: Free starter template

### Paid Plan
- **Mini**: $5/month
- **Features**: Custom domain, 1K form submissions, 5 CMS collections, remove branding
