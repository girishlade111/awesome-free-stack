## Penpot

**Website**: https://penpot.app
**Docs**: https://help.penpot.app
**GitHub**: https://github.com/penpot/penpot
**Category**: design
**Subcategory**: prototyping

### Description
The first open-source design and prototyping platform — a free alternative to Figma. Web-based with SVG-native format, real-time collaboration, design systems, and developer handoff. Self-host or use Penpot Cloud.

### Free Tier
- **Cloud**: Unlimited files, unlimited collaborators, all features
- **Self-hosted**: Completely free forever with no limits

### Paid Plan
- **Price**: No paid version
- **Model**: Penpot Cloud is free, self-hosted is free
