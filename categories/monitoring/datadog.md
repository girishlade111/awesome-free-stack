## Datadog

**Website**: https://www.datadoghq.com
**Docs**: https://docs.datadoghq.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/DataDog
**Category**: monitoring
**Subcategory**: infrastructure-monitoring

### Description
Full-stack observability platform for cloud-scale applications. Combines infrastructure monitoring, APM, log management, real-user monitoring, synthetic monitoring, and security in one unified platform with AI-powered alerts.

### Free Tier
- **Hosts**: 10 (1 day metric retention)
- **Logs**: 5M events/month (3 day retention)
- **Metrics**: 6 months retention
- **Alerts**: Standard alerts included

### Paid Plan
- **Pro**: $15/host/month
- **Features**: Full 15 month retention, APM, advanced alerts, dashboards, team collaboration
