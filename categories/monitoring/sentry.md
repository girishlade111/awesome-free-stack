## Sentry

**Website**: https://sentry.io
**Docs**: https://docs.sentry.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/getsentry/sentry
**Category**: monitoring

### Description
Industry-standard error tracking and performance monitoring platform. Real-time crash reporting with full stack traces, performance spans, sessions, and release health. Open-source SDKs for every language and framework.

### Free Tier
- **Errors**: 5K/month
- **Transactions**: 5K/month (performance)
- **Traces**: 1M/month
- **Users**: 1
- **Retention**: 30 days

### Paid Plan
- **Team**: $26/month
- **Features**: 50K errors, 50K transactions, team collaboration, spike protection
