## Free Tier Details

- **Courses**: 2,500+ MIT courses
- **Materials**: Lecture notes, assignments, exams, solutions
- **Media**: Video lectures for many courses
- **Subjects**: Engineering, CS, Math, Science, Humanities
- **License**: Creative Commons (many courses)

## Pros

- Actual MIT course materials, completely free
- No enrollment or registration required
- Includes solutions and grading rubrics
- Broad subject coverage beyond CS
- High-quality, rigorous content

## Cons

- No instructor interaction or mentoring
- No certificates or credentials
- Self-discipline required to complete
- Some courses have outdated materials
- No structured learning path

## Key Features

- Full semester course materials
- Lecture videos for popular courses
- Assignments with solutions
- Exams with answer keys
- Reading lists and syllabi
- Translated materials for select courses

## Alternatives

- OSSU — structured curriculum using MIT OCW + other materials
- Coursera — structured with certificates
- edX — MIT courses on edX (some free)
