## Free Tier Details

- **Courses**: 7,000+ available for audit
- **Content**: Full lecture videos, readings, discussion forums
- **Limitations**: No graded assignments, no quizzes, no certificate
- **Duration**: Full course duration access
- **Languages**: Multi-language subtitles available

## Pros

- Courses from top universities (Stanford, Yale, MIT, Princeton)
- Industry partners (Google, Meta, IBM, AWS)
- Audit option for free access to all materials
- Recognized certificates and degrees
- Mobile app for learning on-the-go

## Cons

- Certificates cost $49-99 per course
- No graded assignments on free audit
- Specializations can get expensive
- Course quality varies by institution

## Key Features

- Guided Projects (hands-on, 1-2 hours)
- Specializations (multi-course series, $39-79/mo)
- Professional Certificates (job-ready skills)
- Master's Degrees (online, accredited)
- Peer-graded assignments
- Financial aid available

## Alternatives

- MIT OCW — 100% free MIT materials
- edX — similar model, different universities
- Udemy — pay-per-course, frequent sales
