### Description
Global VPS and bare-metal cloud provider with 32 data center locations. Deploy cloud compute, GPU instances, and bare metal servers in minutes with hourly billing.

### Free Tier
- **Credit**: $100 for 30 days
- **No always-free tier**: Credit covers 2+ months of a $6/mo VPS
- **Cheapest plan**: 1 GB RAM, 1vCPU, 25 GB SSD, 1 TB transfer

### Paid Plan
- **Cloud Compute**: $6/month (1 GB, 1vCPU, 25 GB SSD, 1 TB transfer)
- **Bare Metal**: From $120/month
- **GPU**: From $0.95/hour
