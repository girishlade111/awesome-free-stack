## Portainer

**Website**: https://www.portainer.io
**Docs**: https://docs.portainer.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/portainer/portainer
**Category**: hosting
**Subcategory**: vps

### Description
Container management platform with intuitive web UI for Docker, Swarm, and Kubernetes. Community Edition is free with unlimited nodes. Business Edition has a free tier for 5 nodes.

### Free Tier
- **Community Edition**: Free, unlimited nodes, all core features
- **Business Edition free**: 5 nodes
- **Features**: Container management, stacks, templates, RBAC (BE)

### Paid Plan
- **Business**: From $4.50/node/month
- **Features**: RBAC, registry management, GitOps, 24/7 support
