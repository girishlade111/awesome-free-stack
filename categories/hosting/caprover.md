## CapRover

**Website**: https://caprover.com
**Docs**: https://caprover.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/caprover/caprover
**Category**: hosting
**Subcategory**: vps

### Description
Open-source PaaS that turns any VPS into a Heroku-like deployment platform. One-command app deployment with Docker, Let's Encrypt SSL, and built-in CI/CD.

### Free Tier
- **Self-hosted**: Completely free with unlimited apps, domains, and deployments
- **Cost**: Pay only for your VPS or cloud server

### Paid Plan
- **Self-hosted**: Free forever
- **CapRover Cloud**: Coming soon
