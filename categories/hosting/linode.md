### Description
Popular VPS provider known for developer-friendly interface, flat pricing, and excellent documentation. Now part of Akamai. $100 free credit for new users.

### Free Tier
- **Credit**: $100 for 60 days
- **No always-free tier**: Credit covers multiple months of a $5/mo Nanode
- **Nanode specs**: 1 GB RAM, 1vCPU, 25 GB SSD, 1 TB transfer

### Paid Plan
- **Nanode**: $5/month (1 GB, 1vCPU, 25 GB SSD, 1 TB transfer)
- **Higher plans**: Up to Dedicated 64 GB and GPU instances
