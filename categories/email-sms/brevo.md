## Brevo (Sendinblue)

**Website**: https://www.brevo.com

**Subcategory**: email-marketing

### Description
All-in-one CRM platform for email marketing, transactional email, SMS, and live chat. Free plan includes unlimited contacts with daily sending limit. Built for small businesses with drag-and-drop editor, automation, and landing pages.

### Free Tier
- **Emails**: 300/day (9,000/month)
- **Contacts**: Unlimited
- **Templates**: Unlimited email templates
- **SMS**: Test mode credits
- **Extra**: Landing pages, signup forms, segmentation

### Paid Plan
- **Starter**: $25/month
- **Features**: 20K emails/day, no Brevo branding, A/B testing, advanced statistics
