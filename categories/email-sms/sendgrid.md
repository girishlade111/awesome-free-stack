## SendGrid (Twilio)

**Website**: https://sendgrid.com
**Docs**: https://docs.sendgrid.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/sendgrid
**Category**: email-sms

### Description
Industry-leading transactional email platform by Twilio. Send bulk and triggered emails with 99.9% uptime, advanced analytics, dedicated IPs, and 40+ SDKs. Trusted by millions of developers for deliverability at scale.

### Free Tier
- **Emails**: 100/day (3,000/month)
- **Contacts**: 2,000
- **Templates**: 2,000
- **Email validation**: API included
- **Statistics**: Basic analytics

### Paid Plan
- **Essentials**: $19.95/month
- **Features**: 50K emails, 5K contacts, no SendGrid branding, email support
