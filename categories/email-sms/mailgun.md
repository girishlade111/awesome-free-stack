## Mailgun

**Website**: https://www.mailgun.com
**Docs**: https://documentation.mailgun.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/mailgun
**Category**: email-sms
**Subcategory**: email-api

### Description
Powerful email API for developers with advanced deliverability features. Flexible sending options via SMTP or REST API, real-time analytics, email validation, and best-in-class routing. Own your email infrastructure.

### Free Tier
- **Emails**: 5,000/month (first 3 months: 50K)
- **Daily limit**: 20/day after trial
- **Email validation**: 400 free/month
- **Storage**: 1 GB log retention

### Paid Plan
- **Flex**: Starting at $35/month
- **Features**: 50K emails, dedicated IP, custom analytics, advanced routing
