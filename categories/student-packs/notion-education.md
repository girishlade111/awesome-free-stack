# Notion Education

Notion offers a free Plus plan for students and educators.

## Benefits

| Feature | Student Plan | Free Plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Storage** | Unlimited file uploads | 5 MB file uploads |
| **Block Limit** | Unlimited | 7-day page history |
| **Page History** | 30 days | 7 days |
| **Guest Invites** | 100 guests | 10 guests |
| **Custom APIs** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Teamspaces** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Timeline View** | ✅ | ❌ |
| **AI Writing** | Free trial included | ❌ |

## Verification

| Method | Details |
|---|---|
| **Platform** | Notion Education portal |
| **Email** | `.edu` email (instant) |
| **Duration** | 12 months |
| **Renewal** | Renewable while enrolled |

## How to Claim

1. Go to [notion.so/students](https://www.notion.so/students)
2. Sign in with your Notion account
3. Verify with `.edu` email
4. Plus plan upgrades free for 1 year
5. Re-verify annually

## Use Cases for Students

- **Class Notes** — Organize lecture notes in structured pages
- **Project Management** — Track assignments and deadlines
- **Wiki / Knowledge Base** — Collaborative study resources
- **Portfolio** — Build project showcase pages
- **Task Manager** — Personal productivity system
