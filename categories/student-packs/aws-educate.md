## Free Tier Details

- **Credits**: Up to $500+ in promotional credits
- **Labs**: 50+ hours of free hands-on labs
- **Training**: Free AWS training courses
- **Career Pathways**: Guided learning for cloud roles
- **Community**: Student-only forums and events
- **Duration**: Credits valid for 12 months

## Pros

- Generous $500+ in cloud credits
- Hands-on labs for real-world practice
- Career pathways for specific roles
- No credit card required
- Can combine with AWS Free Tier

## Cons

- Credits expire after 12 months
- Some services not covered
- Lab hours may be limited
- Separate from AWS Free Tier

## Key Features

- Promotional credits for AWS services
- Self-paced training courses
- Hands-on lab exercises
- Career pathways (Architect, Developer, DevOps)
- AWS Educate Job Board
- Badges and certifications
