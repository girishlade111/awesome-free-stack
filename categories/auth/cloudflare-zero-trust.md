### Description
Cloudflare Zero Trust replaces conventional SSO with identity-aware access control. Supports OIDC, SAML, social login, device posture checks, and integrates with any identity provider. Free tier for up to 50 users.
### Free Tier
- Free plan: up to 50 users, unlimited network policies, social SSO, email OTP, and basic device posture checks.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $7/month
- Zero Trust plans start at $7/user/month (annual). Includes advanced DLP, CASB, and data protection.
