## Descope

**Website**: https://www.descope.com
**Docs**: https://docs.descope.com
**GitHub**: https://github.com/descope
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: passwordless

### Description
Codeless passwordless authentication and identity platform. Drag-and-drop workflow builder for auth flows with passkeys, magic links, OTP, and biometrics. Includes pre-built login components and step-up MFA.

### Free Tier
- Free tier: 7,500 MAU, unlimited workflows, all auth methods, pre-built components, and community support.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $200/month
- Scale from $200/month for 50K MAU. Enterprise with SSO, SAML, audit logs, and dedicated support.
