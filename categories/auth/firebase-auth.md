## Firebase Auth

**Website**: https://firebase.google.com/products/auth
**Docs**: https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth
**GitHub**: https://github.com/firebase
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: authentication

### Description
Google's production-grade authentication with email/password, 30+ social providers, phone auth, and anonymous sign-in. Tightly integrated with Firebase and Google Cloud ecosystem.

### Free Tier
- **MAU**: 50K (email/password/social)
- **Phone auth**: 10K MAU
- **Verification codes**: 10K/day

### Paid Plan
- **Blaze**: Pay-as-you-go
- **Email MAU**: $0.01/10K beyond free
- **Phone auth**: $0.01/verification
