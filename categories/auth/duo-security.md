## Duo Security
**Website**: https://duo.com
**Docs**: https://duo.com/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/duosecurity
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: mfa
### Description
Cisco-owned MFA platform providing push notifications, TOTP, hardware tokens, and biometric verification. Free tier supports up to 10 users with core MFA features. Integrates via SSO proxies, RADIUS, and direct SDKs.
### Free Tier
- Free plan: up to 10 users, unlimited push and TOTP authentications
- Includes Duo Mobile app, device health checks, and self-service enrollment.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $3/month
- Duo Advantage from $3/user/month. Includes phone support, SSO connectors, directory sync, and advanced policies.
