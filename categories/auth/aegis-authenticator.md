## Aegis Authenticator

**Website**: https://getaegis.app

### Description
Open-source 2FA app for Android with encrypted backups, import/export, and Wear OS support. Supports TOTP and HOTP with icons and categories. Material Design interface with biometric lock and auto-lock.

### Free Tier
- Completely free open-source Android app
