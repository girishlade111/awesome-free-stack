## andOTP

**Website**: https://andotp.github.io

**Category**: auth

### Description
Open-source TOTP authenticator for Android with encrypted backups (OpenPGP), auto-lock, and multi-profile support. Supports time-based and counter-based OTPs with Material Design and dark theme.

### Free Tier
- Completely free open-source Android app
- All features included with no ads or in-app purchases
- Encrypted backups via OpenPGP.
