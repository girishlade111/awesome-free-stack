## Dex
**Website**: https://dexidp.io
**Docs**: https://dexidp.io/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/dexidp/dex
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: sso
### Description
Open-source OIDC identity provider with multiple authentication backends. Originally by CoreOS, Dex connects to LDAP, SAML, GitHub, Google, Microsoft, and other identity providers. Widely used in Kubernetes clusters.
### Free Tier
- Completely free under Apache 2.0 license
- Self-hosted with unlimited users and connectors
- No paid tier exists.
