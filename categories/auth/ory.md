## Ory
**Website**: https://www.ory.sh
**Docs**: https://www.ory.sh/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/ory
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: sso
### Description
Open-source identity infrastructure suite. Includes Ory Hydra (OAuth 2.0/OIDC server), Ory Kratos (identity management), Ory Keto (permissions), and Ory Oathkeeper (reverse proxy). Cloud and self-hosted options.
### Free Tier
- Free managed tier: 10,000 sessions/month, community support
- Self-hosted Ory OSS is completely free under Apache 2.0.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $25/month
- Ory Cloud: $25/month for 100K sessions. Enterprise: custom pricing with SLA and dedicated support.
