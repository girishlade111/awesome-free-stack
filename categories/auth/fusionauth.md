## FusionAuth
**Website**: https://fusionauth.io
**Docs**: https://fusionauth.io/docs
**GitHub**: https://github.com/FusionAuth/fusionauth-containers
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: social-login
### Description
Full-featured auth platform with social login, SSO, MFA, and user management. Supports Google, Apple, Facebook, Twitter, GitHub, LinkedIn, and custom OAuth providers. Free community edition for self-hosting.
### Free Tier
- Free Community Edition: unlimited users and social login providers
- Self-hosted with no feature restrictions
- Paid cloud free tier: 5K MAU.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $75/month
- Cloud from $75/month for 30K MAU. Enterprise with SLA, dedicated support, and advanced compliance.
