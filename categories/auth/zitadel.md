### Description
Cloud-native identity and access management platform built for Kubernetes. Supports SSO, OIDC, OAuth 2.0, SAML, LDAP, multi-tenancy, and audit logging. Offers managed cloud and self-hosted open-source versions.
### Free Tier
- Free tier includes 5,000 monthly active users
- Self-hosted version is free with unlimited users.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $149/month
- Cloud Enterprise: $149/month for 25K MAU. Dedicated and self-hosted enterprise plans available.
