## MiniOrange Social Login
**Website**: https://www.miniorange.com/social-login
**Docs**: https://www.miniorange.com/social-login/documentation
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: social-login
### Description
Social login plugin and API supporting 40+ OAuth providers including Google, Apple, Facebook, LinkedIn, and GitHub. Provides WordPress, Drupal, Joomla plugins plus standalone REST APIs. Free tier for small sites.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: 1 website, unlimited social login buttons, 2 social providers, community support
- Works with popular CMS platforms.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $99/month
- Pro from $99/year for unlimited providers and 3 websites. Enterprise with custom integrations and SLA.
