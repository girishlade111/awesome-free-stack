### Description
Managed SpiceDB service built on Google Zanzibar. Provides relationship-based authorization with a graph database model. Features consistency guarantees, CEL-based schema, and SDKs for all major languages. Free tier available.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: 2 services, 50 relationships/second, 2.5M relationship reads/month
- Self-hosted SpiceDB is unlimited.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $250/month
- Team from $250/month with 10 services and 250 relationships/second. Enterprise custom pricing available.
