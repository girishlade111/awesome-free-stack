## Passage by 1Password
**Website**: https://passage.id
**Docs**: https://docs.passage.id
**GitHub**: https://github.com/passageidentity
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: passwordless
### Description
Passkey authentication service by 1Password. Drop-in passkey components for web and mobile apps with biometric and PIN verification. Supports multi-device passkey sync and FIDO2 WebAuthn standards.
### Free Tier
- Free Developer plan: 1 app, up to 1K users, all passkey features
- Community support on Discord.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $99/month
- Growth from $99/month for 5K users. Enterprise with custom pricing, dedicated support, and SLA.
