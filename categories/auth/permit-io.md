## Permit.io
**Website**: https://www.permit.io
**Docs**: https://docs.permit.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/permitio
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: authorization
### Description
Policy-as-code authorization service with low-code UI. Supports RBAC, ABAC, and ReBAC with attribute-based policies. Includes a visual policy editor, decision logging, and audit trails. Free tier for startups.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: up to 250 users, unlimited policy evaluations, audit logs, and a low-code policy editor
- Community support on Slack.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $17/month
- Pro from $17/month for 2K users. Enterprise with SSO, dedicated infra, and SLA available.
