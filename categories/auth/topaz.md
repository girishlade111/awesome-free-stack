## Topaz

**Website**: https://www.topaz.sh

**Category**: auth

### Description
Open-source authorization service by Aserto based on Google Zanzibar. Provides fine-grained access control with directory, relationship-based authz, and policy-as-code (Rego). Includes a no-code authorizer UI for testing.

### Free Tier
- Completely free open-source version
- Self-hosted or managed via Aserto Free tier: 500 users, unlimited relationships, and policy decisions.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $199/month
- Aserto Pro from $199/month for 5K users. Enterprise with dedicated deployment and SSO.
