### Description
Passkey-first authentication platform for consumer and enterprise apps. Supports WebAuthn passkeys with email fallback. Provides pre-built UI components and backend SDKs. Focused on high passkey conversion rates.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: up to 1,000 passkey users, unlimited transactions, all core features
- No credit card required.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $99/month
- Growth from $99/month for 5K users. Enterprise with custom pricing, SLA, and dedicated passkey infrastructure.
