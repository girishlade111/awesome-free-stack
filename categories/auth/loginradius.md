## LoginRadius
**Website**: https://www.loginradius.com
**Docs**: https://www.loginradius.com/docs
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: social-login
### Description
Social login and customer identity platform supporting 40+ social providers. Features unified login, profile management, and identity federation. Free tier for startups with core social login capabilities.
### Free Tier
- Free startup plan: 10K MAU, unlimited social login providers, 3 apps, community support
- Standard identity management included.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $249/month
- Basic from $249/month for 100K MAU. Enterprise with dedicated support, SSO, and SLA.
