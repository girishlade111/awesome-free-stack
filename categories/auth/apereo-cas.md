### Description
Enterprise single sign-on platform with 20+ years of history. Supports CAS protocol, SAML, OAuth 2.0, OIDC, and WS-Federation. Features MFA, password management, and delegated authentication to social/corporate identity providers.
### Free Tier
- Completely free under Apache 2.0 license
- Self-hosted with unlimited users and protocol support.
