## Google Identity Services
**Website**: https://developers.google.com/identity
**Docs**: https://developers.google.com/identity/sign-in/web
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: social-login
### Description
Google's identity platform providing Sign In with Google, OAuth 2.0, and OIDC integration. Includes One Tap, automatic sign-in, and cross-device sessions. Used by millions of websites for free social login.
### Free Tier
- Completely free for all applications
- No usage limits or payment required
- Includes Sign In with Google, One Tap, and Google OAuth.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: N/A
- No paid version; completely free. Google Workspace customers get additional enterprise controls.
