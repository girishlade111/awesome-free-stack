## Sign in with Apple
**Website**: https://developer.apple.com/sign-in-with-apple/
**Docs**: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/sign_in_with_apple
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: social-login
### Description
Apple's social login service providing OAuth 2.0 authentication with privacy-focused features. Includes hidden email relay, private user IDs, and cross-platform support. Required for iOS apps using third-party social login.
### Free Tier
- Completely free for any app or website
- No usage limits or fees
- Includes user authentication, email relay, and JS SDK.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: N/A
- No paid version; completely free with Apple Developer Program membership (required for iOS distribution).
