### Description
Microsoft's free authenticator app supporting TOTP, push notifications, and passwordless phone sign-in. Works with Microsoft accounts, Azure AD, and any TOTP-compatible service. Available on iOS and Android.
### Free Tier
- Completely free app with unlimited accounts
- Supports TOTP codes, push approval, and passwordless authentication
- No usage limits.
