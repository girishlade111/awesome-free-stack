### Description
Open-source auth platform with social login support for Google, Apple, GitHub, Discord, Facebook, and 20+ providers. Features session management, MFA, passwordless, and user management. Self-hosted or cloud with generous free tier.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: 5K MAU, unlimited social login providers, unlimited APIs
- Self-hosted core is completely free under Apache 2.0.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $50/month
- Pro from $50/month for 30K MAU. Enterprise with dedicated support, SSO, and compliance features.
