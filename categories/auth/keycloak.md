**Website**: https://www.keycloak.org

### Description
Open-source identity and access management by Red Hat. Supports SSO, OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML, LDAP, user federation, social login, MFA, and fine-grained authorization policies. Production-ready with clustering support.
### Free Tier
- Completely free under Apache 2.0
- Self-hosted with no usage limits
- Unlimited users, realms, and identity providers.
