### Description
Google's free TOTP authenticator app generating time-based one-time passwords for 2FA. Works with any service supporting TOTP standards (RFC 6238). Available on iOS and Android. No internet connection required.
### Free Tier
- Completely free app with unlimited accounts
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection
- Open standards compliant (TOTP/HOTP).
