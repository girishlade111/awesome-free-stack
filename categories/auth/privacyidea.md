## privacyIDEA

**Website**: https://www.privacyidea.org
**Docs**: https://privacyidea.readthedocs.io
**GitHub**: https://github.com/privacyidea/privacyidea
**Category**: auth
**Subcategory**: mfa

### Description
Open-source multi-factor authentication server supporting TOTP, HOTP, SMS, email, U2F, WebAuthn, YubiKey, and push tokens. Centralized MFA management with policy engine, audit logging, and self-service portal.

### Free Tier
- Completely free community edition under AGPLv3
- Unlimited users and tokens
- All authentication methods included.

### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: N/A
- Enterprise edition with support subscriptions. Premium features: high availability, push tokens, and phone support.
