### Description
Cloud-native authorization service with directory-based access control. Supports RBAC, ABAC, and ReBAC using Rego policies. Includes a managed directory, policy-aware UI, and real-time authorization audit. Free tier for small teams.
### Free Tier
- Free tier: up to 500 users, 100K policy decisions/month, 1 directory, and community support
- No credit card required.
### Paid Plan
- **Starting Price**: $199/month
- Team plan from $199/month for 5K users. Enterprise with SSO, dedicated support, and custom SLAs.
